FIG.08 — INTO THE MACHINE

ADC: analog → digital

SOUND→ MIC→ ANALOG→ ADC→ DATA → PC
Mic hears smooth sound. ADC samples it — measures the height thousands of times per second — and writes each measurement as numbers. Now the PC can store it.
माइकले smooth आवाज सुन्छ। ADC ले त्यसलाई छिनछिनमा नापेर (sampling) अंकमा लेख्छ — अनि कम्प्युटरले राख्न सक्छ।

Cyan = real wave · amber steps = what ADC keeps. Sampling loses a little — that's the price.

FIG.09 — BACK OUT

DAC: digital → analog

DATA→ DAC→ ANALOG→ SPEAKER→ SOUND
Playback reverses it. DAC rebuilds a smooth voltage from the numbers; the speaker shakes air. You hear music.
बजाउँदा उल्टो हुन्छ — DAC ले अंकबाट smooth भोल्टेज बनाउँछ, स्पिकरले हल्लाएर आवाज निकाल्छ।
ONION TRICK 🧅 (no rot, just remember):
ADC = A→D = record in.  ·  DAC = D→A = play out.
रेकर्ड = ADC, बजाउने = DAC।
