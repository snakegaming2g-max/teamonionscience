6 slides, about 3 minutes. Arrows, dots, or swipe. Full detail lives in steps ①–⑤ below.

SLIDE 1 / 6 — SIGNAL

Signal = change that means something

A signal is a physical quantity that changes with time and carries information. Voice shakes air — that shaking pattern is the signal.

सिग्नल = समयसँग बदलिने, सूचना बोक्ने भौतिक परिमाण।

SLIDE 2 / 6 — ANALOG

Analog: smooth & continuous 〰

Any value inside the range is allowed. Drawn as a smooth sine wave — natural voice, old radio. Peak = top, valley = bottom, amplitude = strength.

लगातार परिवर्तन — जुनसुकै मान, smooth wave।

SLIDE 3 / 6 — DIGITAL

Digital: fixed steps ▓

Only fixed levels — HIGH = 1, LOW = 0. Square-step waveform. This is what computers eat.

Fixed मान मात्र — HIGH = 1, LOW = 0।

SLIDE 4 / 6 — BINARY

Binary: only 0 and 1

1 bit = one digit. 8 bits = 1 byte. Exam rule: n bits → 2ⁿ combinations. 3 bits → 8.

1 bit = एउटा अंक, 8 bit = 1 byte, n bit → 2ⁿ।

SLIDE 5 / 6 — ADC · DAC

Bridges both ways

ADC: analog → digital (recording in). DAC: digital → analog (playing out). A→D in, D→A out.

रेकर्ड = ADC, बजाउने = DAC।

SLIDE 6 / 6 — ON THE WIRE

Send it, survive noise

Source → encode → channel → decode. Noise = junk added; distortion = shape bent. Digital can be regenerated and error-checked.

Noise = फोहोर थपिनु; digital लाई सच्याउन मिल्छ।

FIG.01 — WHAT IS A SIGNAL?

Signal = change that means something

A signal is a physical quantity that changes with time and carries information. Your voice shakes air. That shaking pattern is the signal. Same idea for voltage in a wire or brightness of light — change over time, carrying a message.
सिग्नल भनेको समयसँग परिवर्तन हुने भौतिक परिमाण हो जसले सूचना बोक्छ। बोलेको आवाजले हावा हल्लाउँछ — त्यही हल्लिने pattern नै सिग्नल हो। तारको भोल्टेज वा बत्तीको उज्यालो पनि त्यसैगरी सूचना बोक्छ।
WHY NEEDED? → info has to travelकिन चाहिन्छ? → सूचना पुर्‍याउन
🔊 Sound
Air pressure wobbling over time. बोल्दाको हावा।
⚡ Voltage
Electric level rising/falling in a wire.
💡 Light
Brightness changing — remote, fibre.
📡 Radio wave
Invisible EM wave shaped by voice/music.

The route, always the same

INFO→ SIGNAL→ TRAVEL→ RECEIVE→ INFO BACK

Onion rule #1: if nothing changes with time, it's not a signal. It's just a value sitting there.
